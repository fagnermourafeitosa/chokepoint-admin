## Context & Background

The profile menu of the Admin header shows the account and **Sign out**, and nothing else. A person chooses a workspace on the `/workspaces` page and creates the first one on `/onboarding/workspace`. Both pages are outside the Admin layout. Every workspace has exactly two environments, Sandbox and Production (`backend/docs/authorization-domain-model.md`).

The user asked for an area dedicated to the account of the person: separate from the Admin, with its own navigation, and with the same design system. Its first module is the management of workspaces and their environments. The layout was defined with the user on the prototype on 2026-11-11.

---

## Problem Statement

### 1. No place to manage workspaces

A person cannot create a second workspace, rename a workspace or delete it. The environments of a workspace are visible only as a selector in the Admin header.

### 2. No place to manage the account

A person cannot change their name, see the e-mail they sign in with, or delete their account.

### 3. Workspace selection lives in loose pages

`/workspaces` and `/onboarding/workspace` are single pages with no layout. They do the same job as the account area.

---

## Solution

User decisions of 2026-11-11 (grilling and prototype review).

### Area and navigation

1. The account area is a route group `/account` of the Admin application, with its own layout: the Admin top bar with the wordmark, the word **Account** and the profile menu. It has no workspace selector, no environment selector, no Admin navigation and no Report-only banner. It uses the same session and design system.
2. Its sections are design-system inner tabs (`cp-admin-inner-tabs`) at the top of the page: **Workspaces** (Building2 icon) and **Account info** (UserRound icon). The workspace page keeps the Workspaces tab current.
3. The area needs a session but no chosen workspace. It replaces `/workspaces`: after sign-in, a person with workspaces and none chosen goes to `/account/workspaces`. The first-workspace onboarding of Admin 005 stays (user, 2026-11-11): a person with no workspace goes to `/onboarding/workspace`, as before.
4. The profile menu of the Admin header gets the item **Account**, above Sign out, that opens `/account/workspaces`. The account area keeps the same profile menu.
5. **Open Admin** on a workspace (and on an environment) chooses that workspace (and that environment) and opens the Admin at `/`.

### Workspaces tab (`/account/workspaces`)

6. Page heading **Workspaces**, with "The workspaces you belong to. Each one has a Sandbox and a Production environment." and the primary action **Create workspace**.
7. A table (pattern of the Integrations lists): **Workspace** (Building2 icon, bold name, workspace ID in mono below), **Environments** (neutral badges Sandbox and Production), **Your role** (neutral badge, Admin), **Created** (date), **Actions** right aligned with its header: Open Admin and Edit (ghost, small), Delete (danger-soft, small). The trace pager is below the table.
8. **Create workspace** opens a dialog with **Workspace name** ("Up to 100 characters. The workspace starts with a Sandbox and a Production environment.") and Cancel / Create workspace.
9. **Delete** opens the delete workspace dialog (item 13).

### Workspace page (`/account/workspaces/:tenantId`)

10. Heading with the workspace name, "The name and the environments of this workspace." and **Back to workspaces** (secondary).
11. Section **Name**: "The name that people see in the Admin and on the login pages.", the **Workspace name** field, "Up to 100 characters. Workspace ID <id>".
12. Section **Environments**: "Every workspace has these two environments. Nothing is shared between them." A table with **Environment** (bold name and environment ID in mono), **Authorization server issuer** (mono) and **Actions** (Open Admin). The environments are fixed: no create, rename or delete.
13. Form footer: **Save changes** on the left; **Cancel** and **Delete workspace** (danger-soft) on the right. The delete workspace dialog asks "Delete <name> with its Sandbox and Production environments?", shows a warning notice ("Every agent, key, connection, permission and record of both environments is deleted. Agents of this workspace get no token after this. You cannot undo it.") and enables **Delete workspace** only when the typed text equals the workspace name. The deletion is immediate.

### Account info tab (`/account/info`)

14. Heading **Account info**, "Your name and the email you sign in with."
15. Section **Profile**: "People of your workspaces see this name in the revision history and the Inbox.", **Name** (editable, up to 100 characters) and **Email** (read only, "You sign in with this email."). Footer: **Save changes**.
16. Danger area below the form, after a divider: the title **Delete account** in the danger color, "Deletes your account and your access to every workspace." and the button **Delete account** (danger-soft).
17. The delete account dialog has more urgency than the other confirmations (user decision; only this dialog):
    - a danger notice: "This permanently deletes your account. You cannot undo it." / "You lose access to every workspace at once.";
    - "These workspaces have no other admin. They are deleted with their Sandbox and Production environments, agents, keys, connections, permissions and records:" and the list of those workspaces, each with its name and its agent count ("6 agents in Sandbox and Production"), not its ID;
    - "Agents of these workspaces get no token after this, and their calls are refused.";
    - "Type <email> to confirm", and **Delete account permanently** (danger) enabled only when the text equals the e-mail.
    After the deletion the person goes to the login page.

### Roles

18. The person who creates a workspace is its **Admin**. Today every member is Admin, so every member sees Edit and Delete. A later specification adds a **Developer** role with restricted actions in all the Admin (Further Notes).

## User Stories

- [ ] 1. As a person, I want an account area apart from the Admin, so that account work does not mix with the work of one environment.
- [ ] 2. As a person, I want to open the account area from the profile menu, so that I find it from any Admin screen.
- [ ] 3. As a person, I want to see all my workspaces with their environments, role and creation date.
- [ ] 4. As a person, I want to open a workspace or one of its environments in the Admin.
- [ ] 5. As a person, I want to create another workspace.
- [ ] 6. As an Admin, I want to rename a workspace.
- [ ] 7. As an Admin, I want to delete a workspace with its environments, after I type its name.
- [ ] 8. As a person, I want to see the authorization server issuer of each environment.
- [ ] 9. As a person, I want to change my name and see my sign-in e-mail.
- [ ] 10. As a person, I want to see which workspaces my account deletion removes, and how many agents they have, before I delete my account.
- [ ] 11. As a person with no workspace, I want to create my first workspace on the onboarding page.

## Implementation Decisions

1. Routes `/account/workspaces`, `/account/workspaces/:tenantId` and `/account/info`, under the session guard and outside `AdminLayout`, in an `AccountLayout`. `/account` redirects to `/account/workspaces`. `/workspaces` redirects to `/account/workspaces` and its page is removed. `/onboarding/workspace` stays; after the first workspace it opens the Admin.
2. The session guard sends a person with no workspace to `/onboarding/workspace`, and a person with workspaces and none chosen to `/account/workspaces` instead of `/workspaces`.
3. Reuse only design-system parts and existing patterns: `cp-admin-topbar`, `Wordmark`, `Dropdown` of the profile menu, `cp-admin-inner-tabs`, `cp-admin-table`, `mcp-badge`, `TracePager`, `Dialog`, `cp-admin-notice` (warning; danger only in the delete account dialog), `cp-admin-field`, the button variants. No new component.
4. The delete workspace and delete account dialogs use `Dialog` with a confirmation field, because `ConfirmDialog` has no field.
5. Data and writes come from Backend 084 (Backend dependencies). Name limits follow the backend: trimmed, nonblank, up to 100 characters.
6. Copy never names the product (Admin copy rule).

## Out of Scope

- The Developer role and any difference of actions between roles.
- Inviting members, leaving a workspace, transferring the Admin role.
- Creating, renaming or deleting environments.
- Changing the sign-in e-mail or the password.
- Other account modules (for example billing).

---

## Implementation Notes (2026-11-11)

- The delete account dialog, when no workspace is deleted, says "Every workspace of yours has another admin, so no workspace is deleted." The prototype had no text for this case.
- When the preview fails, the dialog shows "Could not check which workspaces are deleted with your account. Close and try again." and keeps the button disabled.
- After the account deletion the session ends and the Admin shows the login page.
