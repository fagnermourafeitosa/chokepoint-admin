## Context & Background

Security teams investigate incidents in the SIEM they already run. Backend 086 sends each allowed and denied tool call to a destination of the customer, by OTLP or by webhook. The person configures the destinations in the account area of Admin 051, next to the workspaces.

---

## Problem Statement

### 1. No place to send calls to the SIEM

A person cannot configure where the calls of their workspaces go, or see whether the delivery works.

---

## Solution

User decisions of 2026-11-12.

### Tab

1. The account area gets a third tab, **Events export** (Send icon), between **Workspaces** and **Account info**, at `/account/events-export`. Name approved by the user on 2026-11-12 ("Events export"; "logs" only explains it).

### Destination list (`/account/events-export`)

2. Page heading **Events export**, with "Send each allowed and denied call of your workspaces to your SIEM or observability tool, by OpenTelemetry (OTLP) or webhook." and the primary action **Add destination**.
3. A table (pattern of the Integrations and Context sources lists): **Destination** (Send icon, bold name, endpoint in mono below), **Type** (neutral badge OTLP or Webhook), **Workspaces** (a neutral badge for each exported workspace and environment, "Acme Billing · Production"), **Status** (badge Delivering green, Failing red, Disabled neutral, with "Last delivery <date time>" below), **Actions** right aligned with its header: Edit (ghost, small) and Delete (danger-soft, small). The pager is below the table.
4. **Delete** opens the confirmation dialog: "Delete <name>? This removes its settings and its credentials." with the warning that the calls of its workspaces stop going to its endpoint at once and that events not delivered yet are dropped.

### Destination form (`/account/events-export/new`, `/account/events-export/:id`)

5. Heading with the destination name (or **New destination**), "Where the calls go, how they are sent and which workspaces send them." and **Back to destinations**.
6. A failing destination shows a danger notice above the form with the last error and the last delivery.
7. **Enable destination** switch at the top.
8. Section **Connection**: Destination name; **Type** select (OpenTelemetry (OTLP) · log records to a collector or a vendor endpoint / Webhook · a signed JSON POST to your URL).
   - OTLP: **Endpoint URL** (HTTPS only) and **Protocol** select (HTTP/protobuf · port 4318, gRPC · port 4317) on one row.
   - Webhook: **Webhook URL** (HTTPS only) and **Signing secret**: "Created when you save. You see it once." on a new destination; masked with **Rotate secret** on an existing one.
   - **Headers**: rows of Header name and Value (password field, "Saved value. Leave blank to retain.") with Remove, and **Add header**.
9. Section **What is exported**, with no checkboxes and no selects (user, 2026-11-12): a table of every workspace of the account, as the account area shows them today: the workspaces where the person is Admin (user, 2026-11-12), with the columns **Workspace** (Building2 icon, bold name, workspace ID in mono below), **Production** and **Sandbox**, each a switch. Each workspace chooses its own environments, for example one in Sandbox and another in Production. A workspace with both switches off sends nothing. The pager is below the table, as under every Admin list (user, 2026-11-12); switches turned on in other pages stay on when the destination is saved. Helper text: "The workspaces of your account. A workspace with both switches off sends nothing." Rejected on the way: checkboxes, one select per workspace row, a multi-select of workspaces with one environment select for the whole destination (it cannot mix environments).
10. Section **Test**: **Send test event** sends one sample event with the settings of the form, and a notice shows the result.
11. Footer: **Save destination** on the left; Cancel and, on an existing destination, **Delete destination** on the right.

## Implementation notes (2026-11-12)

1. Screens in `src/features/account/EventsExportPage.tsx` (list) and `EventsExportFormPage.tsx` (form), API in `events-export-api.ts`, routes `/account/events-export`, `/account/events-export/new` and `/account/events-export/:id`. The prototype route was removed and the prototype is Archived.
2. After the save of a new webhook, and after **Rotate secret** (confirmation dialog: requests signed with the current secret stop passing the check of the receiver), a warning notice shows the secret once: "Copy the signing secret now. You will not see it again.", with a Copy button.
3. **Send test event** shows an info notice "Delivered. The destination answered <status> in <ms> ms." (no status for gRPC) or an error notice "Not delivered: <short error>.", for example "401 Unauthorized from localhost" or "gRPC Unauthenticated from localhost". The text of the receiver is never shown (Backend 086, Outbound requests 4).
4. The failing notice also says how many older calls were dropped, when any were.
5. A refused endpoint shows "This endpoint is not allowed. Use an https address that resolves to a public address."; a workspace where the person is not Admin shows "You can export only the workspaces where you are Admin."
6. An endpoint written as an IP address is refused by the WAF of Envoy before the backend (Backend 086, Implementation Decision 8), with a 403 and no error code; the form then shows "The request was refused. If the endpoint is an IP address, use its hostname."

7. **Design system** (user, 2026-11-12): the parts new to this screen moved to the design system: `CopyButton` (`src/components/ui/copy`), `OneTimeSecret` (`src/components/ui/secret`, class `cp-admin-secret`) and the switch table (`cp-admin-switch-table`), documented in `docs/design-system/components.md`. The screen reuses the account area classes of `account.css` (name, badges, row actions, footer, mono, table) and keeps in `events-export.css` only what is unique to it. Other screens that copy a value keep their own buttons; moving them to `CopyButton` is not part of this specification.

Functional test (2026-11-12) in the built-in browser on the demo copy of Backend 086, every destination created from the form: OTLP/gRPC with a header, test delivered; edit with the header left blank keeps the stored value; wrong token shows the gRPC code; webhook saved with the secret shown once, test delivered to a receiver that checks the signature, and 401 shown when the receiver has another secret; failing notice; rotate secret; delete from the list; 24 destinations paged 20 and 4; 375 px with no horizontal scroll. Every test destination was deleted at the end.

## Resolved decisions
