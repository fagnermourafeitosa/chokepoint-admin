## Context & Background

Barricade is the security section of a workspace (user decision, 2026-11-16), with its own icon (`src/assets/barricade.svg`: an agent behind a brick wall, guarded by a shield). Its settings belong to a workspace and apply to Sandbox and Production, so it is a tab of the account area, after Events export (user decision, 2026-11-16), not a page of the environment-scoped Admin.

---

## Solution

Draft, from the prototype:

1. Account tabs: Workspaces, Events export, **Barricade**, Account info. Barricade uses the Barricade icon.
2. The page: a workspace select (the workspaces where the person is Admin), then **Behavior drift detection** with the five heuristics of Backend 099 and their defaults, then **Response to a departure**: one select per departure (Spike, New tool, New sequence) with Alert, Suspend the tool for the agent, or Disable the agent. A warning appears when a response blocks calls.
3. Cancel and Save on the right, as an editor of one item (docs/design-system/layouts.md).
